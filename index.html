Craftsmanship

Made slowly,
on purpose.

Every CLAIRA piece — bag, chain or garment — starts the same way: one material, chosen well, worked by hand in small batches by artisans who've done little else for twenty years. No shortcuts, no seasons rushed to shelf.

  • Full-grain leather, sourced in Tuscany
  • Solid, unplated brass and 14k gold hardware
  • Hand-stitched edges, saddle-sewn
  • Numbered, made-to-order runs
