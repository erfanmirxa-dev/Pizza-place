Craftsmanship

Made slowly,
on purpose.

Every CLAIRA bag begins as a single hide of full-grain Italian leather, cut by hand and stitched in small batches by artisans who've done little else for twenty years. Solid brass hardware. No shortcuts, no seasons rushed to shelf.

  • Full-grain leather, sourced in Tuscany
  • Solid, unplated brass hardware
  • Hand-stitched edges, saddle-sewn
  • Numbered, made-to-order runs
